# B70 engine opportunities — 2026-09-26

**Research-only decision; no new local GPU benchmark or public speed claim.** This is a follow-up to [RL-073](../../registry/research-ledger.json), the [external sweep](external-lever-sweep-20260926.md), and the [measured outcome ledger](../../docs/plans/2026-09-20-b70-improvement-roadmap/OUTCOMES.md). Numbers below are *external B70 results* unless marked as existing local observations. Different engine/model/quant/context/metric cells must never be compared as if they were one A/B.

## Decision at a glance

- **Largest new, measured relative gain for an untested route on this host:** merged llama.cpp [#28796](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/pull/28796), opt-in sparse FlashAttention for `qwen4exp` QSA at **occupied long context**. Its [author's matched B70 comparison](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/discussions/28695) measured **6.01 → 14.59 tok/s (+143%) at 88.4K** on Flash-Next Q4_K_XL with q8_0 KV; **11.25 → 17.09 (+52%) at 28.6K**, but only **18.15 → 18.44 (+1.6%) at 7.1K**. Those are per-model decode results, not a forecast for this host's two-card/CPU-offloaded placement. The source does not give a transferable host/placement match. This addresses *Flash-Next long-context capacity usability*, not the present Qwen3.8-27B GPTQ speed route. The locally measured Flash-Next p512/g128 baseline is **21.6 engine tok/s with ~194 cold-input tok/s**, dual B70 49/51 plus CPU expert/PLE placement (RL-070 evidence (`../../results/2026/09/flashnext-c1-screen-20260925T215251Z/RESULTS.md`)); an attention-only change cannot promise to eliminate CPU offload or the short-context gap.
- **Best narrow single-B70 dense GGUF decode candidate:** merged llama.cpp [#26689](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/pull/26689) changes quantized-KV Q<=2 decode from VEC to TILE on Xe2. An [independent Qwen3.8 Q4_0 B70 A/B](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/pull/26689#issuecomment-5324067571) measured 6.84 → 12.30 tok/s at **32K actual depth, no speculation**. PR author's model/KV results span +42% to +169% at 32K–118K **without MTP**. This is not a 2x forecast with MTP/DFlash: verification with Q>=3 was already TILE, and a separate [DFlash n2 B70 A/B](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/pull/26689) reported about 0% end-to-end at 64K because the dispatch did not change on its dominant calls. Our general `~/llama.cpp` source still returns VEC for quantized K/V Q<=2; that is *not proof* the separately pinned `qwen4exp` executable lacks the PR. A no-spec long-depth A/B must establish engagement before spending time on speculative serving.
- **vLLM verdict for the retained single-card GPTQ+MTP4 route:** no newly found, demonstrated larger C1 decode gain than the already measured draft-only INT4 +39.6%/+48.6% ([RL-035](../../registry/research-ledger.json), [serving dossier](../../models/qwen/qwen3.8-27b/serving.md)). [RL-072](../../registry/research-ledger.json) traced a warmed round: 87.7% GEMM, remainder about 5 ms, no large unassigned host/attention bucket. The shortlist [#58578](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/issues/58578) shows +25% on someone else's AutoRound/eager workload but the same mechanism failed our finalist [RL-074](../../registry/research-ledger.json); the own-corpus retry failed its thermal gate [RL-077](../../registry/research-ledger.json). Matched TP2 and allgather overlay lost against one card [RL-075](../../registry/research-ledger.json). Do not re-label upstream claims as an unmeasured champion-stack win.
- **Quality first:** the current GPTQ checkpoint deterministically answered 30 rather than 14 for `sum(i*i for i in range(4))` on the live route ([RL-073](external-lever-sweep-20260926.md#3-conflict-found-their-lab-quality-rejects-our-gptq-champion--reproduced-tonight)). Speed rows still stand, but before calling any successor a coding recipe, compare exact-answer task outputs and failure rate to a same-source reference under matched sampling. Do not pin the known-wrong output as the expected *correct* answer.

## Not a new speed queue

- Existing oneDNN/XMX FA [#25222](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/pull/25222) reported 171→730 tok/s prefill at 80K in Qwen3.6 Q8_0 with **F16 KV**, but general local llama.cpp source already has oneDNN selection for eligible Q>=32 and our Flash-Next route uses quantized KV. Before any rebuild, inspect the exact pinned binary, its build flags, and whether the target shape engages it. Do not count the 4.26x as an additional Flash-Next or Qwen3.8 GPTQ gain.
- Multi-column SYCL MMVQ [#21845](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/pull/21845) gave +59.5% speculative decode on Qwen3.6 dense Q4_K_XL externally, **merged June 5**, and general local source contains multi-column dispatch; no new optimization on that already present path. Other models/MoE paths are outside its proof.
- [vLLM #56096](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/56096) fused QK-norm/RoPE/gate B70 region-time change is Qwen3.5-9B; RL-063 checked no `qwen3_5` dispatch for the retained Qwen3.8 route. [#56577](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/56577) draft FP8 head is **CUDA/GB10/Qwen4Exp**, not XPU, and the local draft-only INT4 already covers the same cost class. [#54897](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/54897) NVFP4 hybrid head is NVIDIA SM120 and approximate; not a B70 route.
- [vllm-xpu-kernels #570](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm-xpu-kernels/pull/570) exposes only **+1.7%** relative to tuned graph-capable oneCCL for its Qwen3.8 TP2 example; it requires system-scope peer atomics. Our host reports no remote atomics and matched TP2 already failed (RL-059/075). [RagingNoper's](https://github.com/RagingNoper/qwen36-b70) >190 tok/s TP2 MoE uses another model/quant/topology and a custom all-reduce, not a transferable two-card Qwen3.8 speed target here.
- External per-request numbers must not be mixed with aggregate multi-user throughput (e.g. [Backend.AI GPT-OSS 20B C32 1,334 tok/s](https://www.backend.ai/blog/engineering/2026-06-intel-arc-pro-b70-meets-backend-ai)); the latter is not one user's decode rate.

**Ordering rule:** priority 0 protects correctness; 1 has the largest verified external long-context delta on a model this host serves; 2 is a low-scope single-card/GGUF test; 3 is the serious vLLM alternative *if* quality passes; 4 is a correctness/compatibility update, not a speed prediction. Do not run 1/2 in a live service window without operator-approved isolation and the existing thermal/storage gates. Publish no speed record until measured locally, quality-qualified, and passed through the cookbook evidence process.
