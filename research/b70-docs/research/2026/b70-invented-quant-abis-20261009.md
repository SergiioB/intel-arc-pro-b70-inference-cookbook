**INFERRED rejection of attractive shortcuts:**
- Pre-swizzling int4 can remove some moves, not the need to unpack to bytes for **INT8** DPAS/DP4A. Native mixed integer operand modes require their own ISA proof; they are not a zero-unpack INT8 layout. We deliberately specify byte reconstruction.
- Doubled E2M1 uses signed values up to±12. A signed four-bit two's-complement nibble cannot represent that set directly. A custom four-bit code still needs nonlinear remap. M5X explicitly pays the fifth bit.
- Power-of-two scales do not make arbitrary integer partial sums globally shift-add compatible for free. Common-exponent alignment costs range/rounding/overflow; FP epilogues still need scaling. DSM uses FP16 scales to avoid a gratuitous quality restriction. M5X retains E8M0 because it is native to its source artifact, not because scale work vanishes.

## Common ABI conventions and accounting

**SPEC-PROPOSED:** little-endian. Each tensor has one64B descriptor: bytes0..3 magic (`DSM1`, `M5X1`, `Q3DT`);4 version=1;5 variant;6..7 flags=0;8..15 N(u64);16..23 K(u64);24..31 payload_offset(u64);32..39 scales_offset(u64);40..47 auxiliary_offset(u64);48..55 logical_payload_length(u64);56..63 zero. Offsets absolute from tensor-record start; align plane starts to64B and zero-fill padding. N padded to16; K padded to128 for DSM and32 for M5X; Q3DT uses complete existing256-weight blocks and groups32 blocks, preserving tensor shape. Invalid flags/version/nonfinite scales fail closed. Descriptor/alignment/tail bytes are **additional** to asymptotic table; real tensor census must count them.

**SPEC-PROPOSED logical lane layout:** W[n,k], microtile N16×K32. Define `j=64*floor(k/4)+4*n+(k%4)` for local n∈[0,15], k∈[0,31]. This is a fully specified K4×N16 byte-vector order: low bit/byte index first. It is the intended logical INT8 B feed order, **not a claim that the compiler or GRF source-region ABI accepts a block load without moves**. ISA analyst must map this to actual DPAS operand regions. Global microtiles use K32-major then N16-major order: index `(k_tile*(Npad/16)+n_tile)`. Scale planes are K-group-major then n. Each plane's packed element0 occupies low bits first; no opaque vendor swizzle or runtime lane lookup is permitted.

**INFERRED economics:** table assumes exactly27B dense active weights or3B active of30B fully resident MoE, all weights in the named representation, one cold target read at M1. Excludes head/embedding exceptions, descriptor/padding, KV, activations, draft, writes and launches. These are **GB/target pass = GB/token only at yield1**. M4 expert union may approach4× active traffic; divide a measured round byte count by `y=1+sum(prefix survivals)`, not by4 or by acceptance alpha. For DSM mixed tensors, fractions are weight-weighted separately for dense, active-MoE and resident-MoE; 80/20 is an illustration, not a census.

## 1. DSM: Dual-Scale Margin-allocated bitplanes

### Exact representation

**SPEC-PROPOSED:** one bit-width b∈{3,4} for every quantized block of a tensor, chosen offline; no data-dependent per-block mode. Each G128 row group has two finite nonnegative binary16 scales `s,t` (4B). Per-weight code represents

`w=(-1)^z * (s*u+t*h)`

where z,h∈{0,1}, u∈[0,2^(b−2)−1]. DSM3 gives magnitudes `{0,s,t,s+t}`; DSM4 gives `{0,s,2s,3s,t,t+s,t+2s,t+3s}`. Duplicated signed zero is canonicalized to z=0. Require t≥(2^(b−2)−1)*s to order magnitudes; scales zero allowed. This is a factorized nonlinear scalar grid, not a vector lattice/trellis.

Each N16×K32 microtile has three planes: U packed(b−2) bits/j, H one bit/j, Z one bit/j. DSM3 sizes64+64+64=192B; DSM4 sizes128+64+64=256B. Within a tensor payload, concatenate full U plane across microtiles, then full H plane, then Z plane (each start64B-aligned); offsets computed from padded tile count and b, no per-tile header. Separate scale plane has pairs(s,t) in K128-major,n-major order. Four adjacent K32 tiles share those scales. Total per N16×K128 block: payload768/1024B +64B scales =832/1088B for2048w, hence3.25/4.25bpw.

### Execution, resources and issue price

**INFERRED M1 DP4A:** extract U,H,Z; form signed-byte U' and H'. Compute **two** A8 dot streams, then `a_scale*(s*dotU+t*dotH)` per group. Initial budget12–20 SIMD16-equivalent preparation ops/32w:4–8 extract/expand,4–8 sign/mask handling,4 scale/partial handling allowance. Dots and loads excluded. Costs exceed L4–8; comparable to U10–20, but no dependent codebook gather. Two DP4A streams are explicitly charged, not hidden as unpack. A8 quantization/sums add separately. M1 A16 fallback reconstructs two scaled terms and vector FMA, keeping activation precision.

**INFERRED M4 DPAS:** construct two K32×N16 byte tiles, issue two INT8 DPAS per512 weights (RC4), group-scale and combine partials. Same12–20 preparation estimate amortized across rows if weights reused; group epilogue scales with M. Relative to single-stream INT8, dot work doubles; conservatively price F=4*Pactive*M against363TOPS. An FP16 path reconstructs W and uses two K16 FP-DPAS per512weights, F=2*Pactive*M at compatible FP16/BF16 class. Small-M integer path is an option, not required for representation validity.

**INFERRED prefill:** inline reconstruction into FP16 tiles may beat dual-dot INT8 at large M; tiled dequant once can amortize U/H/Z handling but costs writes+reads and VRAM. Never stage an entire27B/30B model to54/60GB BF16. Two integer B tiles=1024B per SG plus two M×N accumulator streams (512B for M4×N16×4×2), scales and A. 192KB unified L1/SLM is not a guaranteed SLM allocation budget; avoid mandatory SLM and query later. GRF pressure/occupancy and two DPAS streams are primary contention;128-GRF target,256 only after occupancy/spill evidence. No32-WG residency claim.

### Quality, provenance, novelty and kill

**INFERRED quality mechanism:** two independently fitted spacing parameters preserve a dense center and a separated outer cluster rather than one affine grid. This is a small, cheap nonlinear codebook; it does **not** inherit EXL3 vector-error shaping. Train s,t and code assignments with activation/Hessian-weighted error, plus margin-sensitive calibration loss. Allocate DSM4 to entire high-sensitivity tensors and DSM3 elsewhere using a disjoint calibration census; protect head/norm/embeddings at source precision outside the illustrative all-weight row. EXL3/IQ3 precedents motivate testing nonlinear distributions, not predicted survival. Failed RL-113/114/115 repair transfer makes margin allocation a diagnostic hypothesis only. QAT can train directly through this exact two-component grid with separate A8-aware fake quant; no QAT checkpoint is claimed.

**INFERRED conversion recipe, future only:** start from an **existing canonical BF16 artifact**, not dequantized failed GPTQ or EXL3 approximation. Identify one resident BF16 tensor class and immutable hash before authorizing reads/conversion; current host-state warns old BF16 shard links may be broken. Fit DSM3 and DSM4 to that tensor with frozen calibration statistics; independent CPU decoder must reconstruct exactly the prescribed s*u+t*h numerical reference. CPU dot decomposition must match integer dot sums exactly before FP epilogue. Float comparison uses the specified accumulation order, not an invented BF16 parity claim. Allocate bits from calibration margins; evaluate untouched held-out positions. If no valid BF16 source exists, this arm is **BLOCKED on source artifact**, not permission to requant the failed quant. No real tensor converted here.

**INFERRED novelty vs neighbors:** unlike GPTQ's affine grid, IQ's table lookup and EXL3's trellis, this ABI co-designs two additive magnitude bases, Xe2 byte-feed bitplanes, G128 dual-scale plane and tensor-constant margin allocation. Additive quantization and mixed precision themselves already exist; only this precise economical factorization/ABI is proposed new. Hypothesized upstream barrier: two dot streams, no standard loader ABI, and no established quality advantage over mature codebooks.

**Kill criteria:** any CPU bitplane/dot mismatch kills implementation; inability to locate canonical source stops conversion. DSM3/4 must meet lead-frozen quality gate with no exact-task regressions and no degradation at held-out near-boundary decisions beyond the predeclared budget; training-only repaired margins are not a pass. Reject this grid if it requires>4.125 weight-weighted bpw and cannot price≥10% whole-task gain after dual-dot/activation costs. Reject M4 integer branch independently on A8 quality failure, integer overflow, spills erasing the gain, or decoded-tile moves exceeding proposed issue budget. Never widen gates after failure.

## 2. M5X: exact E2M1 recode to signed-five split planes

### Exact representation and execution

**SPEC-PROPOSED:** for native MXFP4 code c, define `v=sign(c)*[0,1,2,3,4,6,8,12][c&7]`. Store five-bit two's-complement v, not its original E2M1 index. Logical w=v*2^(e−128), where e∈[0,254] is the original E8M0 byte (original scale2^(e−127), and v=2*E2M1). e=255 rejected. Signed zero is canonicalized; this preserves real numeric zero, **not signed-zero bit identity**. FP16 underflow/overflow and rounding must follow the original decoder's policy; accepting real-value equality is not sufficient for a cast-order-sensitive kernel.

For N16×K32 tile, low4bits(v) packed low-nibble-first in j order =256B; highbit(v) bitplane=64B; scale plane16 E8M0 bytes, K32-major,n-major. Full low plane then high plane then scale plane, each base64B-aligned.336B/512w=5.25bpw. Decode `x=low|(high<<4); v=(x^16)-16`. No LUT or nonlinear magnitude remap; still extraction, merge and sign extension.

**INFERRED M1 DP4A / M4 INT8 DPAS:**8–12 preparation ops/32w, approximately4–6 low/high extraction,2 merge,2–4 sign extension/operand preparation; add G32 scale partials and activation quantization explicitly. One integer dot stream; M4 one DPAS RC4 per512weights. Group partial scaled by activation_scale×2^(e−128). No shift of each A8 value and no cross-exponent integer accumulator. FP fallback converts v and applies scale feeding FP16 DPAS or vector math. Scale granularityG32 quadruples partial-boundary frequency vs G128. A8 required on integer path, distinct quality gate. Prefill may dequant tile into FP16 then XMX, or integer feed after A8 gate; identical missing-kernel caveat. One512B byte tile +256B M4 integer accumulator +16B scale vector before A/epilogue/temporary registers; highplane loads and highbit expansion compete for issue slots.

### Quality and whether the byte premium is rational

**INFERRED quality mechanism:** exact recoding of a **native MXFP4/QAT artifact** changes no finite weight value under agreed zero/cast semantics. This is a stronger weight-quality argument than designing a new post-train grid, but applies to that source model, not to the failed dense27B W4A16 model. Converting arbitrary27B BF16 to MXFP4 is **not** the proposed recipe and would repeat a quality-failed class. A16 execution can preserve source values; A8 changes activations and never inherits source quality.

**INFERRED conversion/falsifier:** take one expert gate/up tensor from an existing native MXFP4 artifact, decode every16 possible code and all finite scale encodings on CPU, repack low/high planes, then compare to the original decoder's exact FP16/BF16 results including zero and exponent edges. Unsupported exceptional encodings fail closed. CPU integer dot vs scalar exact dot on extrema must match; K32 max absolute partial≤32*127*12=48,768, safely INT32. Full-K sums require group-boundary handling; do not merge arbitrary exponents. The retained synthetic checker proves only integer recode, not artifact floating rounding parity.

**INFERRED novelty:** closest to MXFP4 with a cached INT8 expansion, but retains5 rather than8 payload bits and separates the fifth plane in a specified Xe2 feed order. Not a disguised “DPAS-ready nibble.” New storage/tradeoff ABI, no new learned quantizer. Hypothesized upstream absence:24% more total bytes than MXFP4 and no native signed5 operation; most portable runtimes prefer4-bit storage or full8-bit predecode.

**INFERRED kill:** M5X/MXFP4 bytes=5.25/4.25=1.235294. Need≥23.53% higher effective bandwidth merely to break even at equal work/yield. To reach10% weight-rate gain need ratio≥1.358824. At an MXFP4 baseline>492.19GB/s even the608 pin cannot yield equal weight-only time; at>447.44GB/s the10% rate gate is pin-impossible. Against4.125bpw@430, match requires547.27GB/s and10% requires602GB/s: **near-pin, low confidence**. Kill as a speed arm if such gain cannot be attributed to removed remap cost; keep only exact-layout functionality if explicitly useful. Kill weight parity on any finite-value/cast mismatch; A8 failure kills integer arm, not exact A16 recode. Native-source artifact missing => blocked, no download authorized.

## 3. Q3DT: lossless IQ3 dual-tier transposed delta container

### Exact hot and cold layout

**SPEC-PROPOSED:** preserve existing IQ3_XXS **entire98-byte canonical block exactly**, including its2-byte scale and96B code/sign/subscale representation. No reinterpretation or new weight quantization. Upstream decoder version/table hash is an out-of-band immutable artifact identity; reject mismatched tables. Group32 contiguous canonical256-weight blocks =8192w. Hot GPU panel stores `H[32*p+j]=canonical_block[j][p]`, p=0..97,j=0..31, total3136B. Thus scale low/high byte planes occupy H[0:32],H[32:64]; code plane beginsH[64]. This is an exact byte transpose, **not** a claim these vector-grid codes are DPAS-ready nibbles. End padding uses zero canonical blocks and an external valid-element count from tensor dimensions.

Cold host/PCIe record has16B header:0..3=`Q3DT`;4 version1;5 mode0 raw or1 delta-sparse;6..7 block_count32(u16);8..11 compressed_payload_len(u32);12..15 raw_len3136(u32). Mode0 payload=H3136B. Mode1 forms `D[32*p+j]=H[32*p+j] XOR (H[32*p+j−1] if j>0 else0)`. Store392B zero/nonzero bitmap, low-bit-first in flat D order; append nonzero D bytes in increasing position. Decoder scans mask, expands zeroes and prefix-XORs each32-byte plane. Choose mode1 only if392+nnz<3136; otherwise raw. No general entropy library or hidden dictionary needed. Stream records are byte-contiguous, **not individually64B-padded**; separate u64 offsets per expert/tensor, not per panel, permit cold stream traversal. Each complete record reconstructs exactly one fixed-offset hot panel; no races or data-dependent device allocation.

A cold compression payload ratio c excludes16B record header: `b_cold=3.0625*c+0.015625`. Illustration c=.75 =>2.3125bpw. Worst case raw3.078125bpw, overhead0.51% vs canonical. Dictionary/entropy coding is deliberately not assumed: high-entropy IQ3 codes may defeat this simple compressor.

### Execution and cost

**INFERRED hot M1 DP4A:** reconstruct canonical IQ3 vector/sign codes from transposed loads, use existing pinned codebook semantics to obtain signed bytes, then dot with Q8_1/A8. Budget16–32 SIMD16-equivalent preparation ops/32w **plus** codebook loads; U-class, not L. Transpose may reduce metadata gathers but does not eliminate vector-grid decode. Scale/sign/subscale treatment must reproduce source decoder; exact DP4A parity can retain source activation quantization where already present. M4 proposed INT8 DPAS uses same reconstruction into B bytes,16–32 prep ops/32w plus codebook traffic, with per-subgroup scale partials. Existing Strata A16 path instead reconstructs FP16 and GEMMs; preserve it for quality baseline. For both, table locality competes with GRF/SLM/L1; prefill dequant once per tile and amortize across rows, or preserve existing staged path, counting temporary writes/reads.

**INFERRED cold-to-hot decode:** mask prefix scan, scatter expansion and XOR prefix chain happen on misses, not every weight reuse. Extra issue/sends and serialization are **unpriced**, separately measured from hot U-class count. At M1 and M4 a miss first pays cold transfer + decode + hot write, then hot read unless a proven fused path removes it. GPU hot writes alone add H bytes per miss; cold records require compressed temporary memory. CPU expansion before PCIe would forfeit wire compression; that is only a correctness control, not the claimed benefit. Raw mode should bypass mask decode with a copy. No CPU expert-ID roundtrip introduced inside graph; buffers and offsets preallocated.

**INFERRED serial miss inequality:** for wire ratio r, original transfer bytesDcold, `Tdecode+Tunhidden_extra_write < (1−r)*Dcold/Bpcie` merely to win. No overlap credit without trace. At cited236MB/round and11.5GB/s the floor is20.522ms; c=.75 => wire ratio2.3125/3.0625=.755102, saving **5.026ms maximum** before decode and writes. For135×1.75MB exact product236.25MB, original20.543ms and saving5.031ms. At c≈.665 (about−33% wire), ideal saving≈6.8ms, comparable to cited20.6→13.8ms sensitivity, **not measured compression**.

**Critical residency distinction:** hot bytes remain IQ3_XXS. This design **cannot claim59→87.5% resident experts** merely because cold records shrink. That earlier audit benefit belonged to actually smaller resident IQ2 weights. Q3DT improves pinned-host footprint/PCIe if compressible; optionally holding cold records on GPU consumes capacity and needs a separate cache model. No free resident-slot increase. The second card can store immutable cold records and supply copy-only peer transfers, but11–12GB/s peer copy is not additive608GB/s, and atomics-dependent collectives remain prohibited. Default is independent cards; dual-card tier is optional and unpriced.

### Quality, provenance, novelty and kill

**INFERRED quality mechanism:** unlike DSM it preserves every IQ3 source byte; no approximation-of-approximation. A16 numeric reconstruction and original scale/codebook semantics remain unchanged. It inherits only the source's narrowly observed three exact MoE cases, not broad quality approval and not EXL3's dense pass. Existing A8 MMVQ parity can be exact against that route; Strata A16→A8 is a new quality axis. No claim to rescue failed dense GPTQ. Dense byte arithmetic is illustrative only.

**INFERRED conversion/falsifier:** choose one existing IQ3_XXS MoE gate/up tensor, record its hash and decoder/table revision; transpose blocks, delta-encode without changing any weight bits, then reverse and require byte-for-byte equality including scale bytes and source hash. CPU-reference vector dots using original decoder must match after reconstruction. Scan cold compression over disjoint expert classes, not just an easy selected tensor. This is lossless transcode, exempt in mechanism from the rejected lossy requant-from-quantized idea; still no real conversion authorized in this E0 task.

**INFERRED novelty:** IQ3 itself is upstream; new element is this exact hot byte-plane ABI paired with independently decodable32-block XOR/bitmap cold pages and cache-tier transitions. Generic delta codecs and heterogeneous caches are prior art. No assertion of a new mathematical codebook. Hypothesized upstream barrier: near-entropy-coded weights may be incompressible; variable cold records complicate capture/miss handling; hot transpose requires backend-specific decoder changes. Existing IQ3 engines do not establish this exact container without source proof.

**Kill criteria:** any lossless roundtrip mismatch kills format implementation. If retained one-class census has c>.90, stop the compression-speed branch pending a genuinely different codec (no lowered threshold); even c=.90 saves<2ms of the cited miss floor. At c=.75, kill if decode+unhidden write≥5.026ms/round in that exact sensitivity cell. Whole-task≥10% must be separately priced; on56ms round,5.026ms removal alone gives1.0986×, just **under10%** before decode overhead. Do not promise a10% task win at this point. A claimed hot-layout gain needs matched captured bandwidth/issue evidence; if hot preparation exceeds32ops/32w or reduces occupancy enough to erase it, reject the hot speed claim while retaining proven lossless compression results.

## Integer accuracy contract and relation to existing mechanisms

**SPEC-PROPOSED integer branch contract:** symmetric A8 activation group32: finite A16 inputs, FP32 `a=max(abs(A))/127`; all-zero group uses a=1 and q=0; otherwise round-to-nearest-ties-even(A/a), clamp to[-127,127]. Retain a as FP32, never silently narrow it. Process K32 groups in increasing K, exact INT32 dot, convert partial to FP32, multiply by explicitly FP32-expanded weight/activation scales, then FP32 add in increasing group order; final output cast once. DSM's s,t remain constant across four such groups but activation scales can differ: do not merge those INT32 accumulators across activation scale boundaries. Compute each group's FP32 s*dotU + t*dotH in that stated order before activation scaling; no fused reassociation without a new parity bound. M5X likewise scales each K32 partial. Q3DT must further split at every source subgroup-scale boundary, following its pinned decoder. Source-code verification of those boundaries is a precondition, not an assumed uniform G256 dot. Reject any compiler reassociation or overflow relative to this reference. M1/M4 exact integer partial parity must be bitwise; FP output matches this ordered CPU reference under the specified float semantics. This reference establishes kernel correctness only, **not equivalence to original A16 inference**.

**INFERRED bounds:** DSM4 per32 group |dotU|≤32*127*3=12,192 and |dotH|≤4,064; M5X≤48,768. For finite un-clipped values, A8 rounding contributes |delta y|≤sum_groups(a_group/2)*sum_group|w|, before weight and FP rounding error; compute the actual per-output bound on CPU, with FP32 rounding bounded per operation or exact ordered float reference. FP32 scale underflow/overflow/NaN are explicit fail-closed edge cases. A quality gate must separately test this A8 approximation against A16 at held-out low-margin positions and complete tasks. This is necessary because the reported historical MMQ accuracy failure is not diagnosed by merely proving small INT32 range.

**Parent literature handoff, unverified online in this task:** [AQLM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.06118) is prior art for additive codebooks; DSM intentionally chooses scalar two-basis algebra instead of multi-table lookups. [QuIP#](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.04396) is prior art for Hadamard/incoherence+lattice coding; DSM chooses K-major feed and **no online FWHT**, so it cannot claim that quality benefit without separately paying transform traffic/issue and adding a transform descriptor. [SqueezeLLM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.07629) and [SpQR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.03078) already use nonuniform/outlier structures; DSM intentionally avoids sparse per-weight residual dispatch to retain fixed streaming planes. MX scale/code definitions are existing standards, and upstream MXFP4 MMVQ/multicol and expert reordering mean neither basic B70 FP4 support nor generic pre-swizzling is novel. The new proposal is the signed-five payload and exact specified planes; it is not integer-DPAS-direct without expansion. Q3DT's cold stream is **lossless**, deliberately not IQ2_XXS (66B/256=2.0625bpw) or IQ2_S(82B/256=2.5625bpw): choosing those as cold storage would change weights on cache misses or require impossible exact IQ3 reconstruction. A lossy IQ2 cold tier is a distinct quality-gated proposal, not a valid compression anchor for this design.

## Ranking: Pareto fronts, not a fabricated scalar score

**INFERRED:** multiplying bytes by ops/32w is a screening score, not the execution law; taking bytes×ops/byte just yields ops. R_issue and quality success probability are unmeasured. Therefore no numeric “dominance×quality” scalar is defensible.

1. **Q3DT first for a cheap decision, MoE only:** strongest preservation argument (bit-exact source), no new quantizer; hot3.0625bpw/U-class and cold compression could target measured PCIe floor. CPU compression/roundtrip census can kill it cheaply. It does not dominate DSM's issue cost and may fail entropy immediately. Dense27B not supported by the cited quality artifact.
2. **DSM second as the genuinely new quantizer:**3.25 or3.45 illustrative bpw, cheaper factorized reconstruction than dependent vector/trellis decode is plausible but two dots matter.
