# P3 worklog — entry probe, 2026-09-21

Phase: P3 (K216 grouped-IQ3 prefill). Status entering: `PLANNED`. Artifact: the re-downloaded
83 GiB REAP-K216 UD-IQ3_XXS (`/mnt/<nvme-disk>/models/DeepSeek-V4-Flash-0731-REAP-K216-UD-IQ3_XXS/`).

## Why this probe, and the criterion fixed before measuring

P3's premise is that prefill is limited by the per-ubatch fixed cost — host counting-sort
grouping plus IQ3→FP16 expansion (≈100 MB/op ≈ 5.2× the IQ3 bytes) feeding oneMKL — rather than
by GEMM math. The cheapest falsifier is the ubatch sensitivity: if that fixed cost dominates,
prefill speed must rise materially with the ubatch. Thresholds were fixed in the harness header
before the run: **proceed if ub 512 → 1024 ≥ 1.15×; withdraw if ≤ 1.05×.**

Run: `scripts/tmp/p3-prefill-ub-20260921.sh` (llama.cpp SYCL, pinned `1692f9e50` lineage, dual
card, `-sm layer -ncmoe 15 -ts 65,35 -c 8448 -ngl all -fa on -ctk f16 -ctv f16 -t 8`, mmap load,
230 W declared, one discarded same-shape warmup per config, n=3 cold p7000/g1 with unique
leading nonces, `cache_prompt=false`). The device-ID patch in that build is decode-only
(`ne12 == 1` gate), so prefill is unaffected by it and these numbers hold for either build.

## Result — the premise holds

| config | engine pp (3 samples) | median | vs ub512 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `-b 512 -ub 512` | 98.01 / 101.20 / 101.22 | **101.20** | — |
| `-b 1024 -ub 1024` | 129.38 / 131.36 / 130.78 | **130.78** | **1.292×** |

**1.292× ≥ 1.15× → the entry gate passes and P3 proceeds.** This independently reproduces the
recorded ubatch sensitivity (1.26–1.30×) on the re-downloaded artifact, at a different context
config, which is the strongest available evidence that the grouping/expansion path — not the
GEMM math — is the prefill constraint.

Refreshed prefill anchors for matched use (p7000/g1, `-c 8448`): **101.2 pp at ub512**,
**130.8 pp at ub1024**. The recorded anchor (105.1 pp) was a different cell
(`-b 8192 -ub 1024`, p8192/g1) and must not be compared directly.

## Attribution step — the routed path IS the prefill wall, at ~25× off dense-GEMM efficiency

Measured at the prefill regime with the same operator harness (216 experts, top-6, hidden 4096,
expert ffn 2048; `n` = ubatch tokens; three repetitions):

| op | n=32 (screen regime) | **n=512 (prefill regime)** |
|---|---:|---:|
| gate/up (`m=2048, k=4096`) | 6.28 ms (0.51 TFLOPS) | **28.28 ms (1.82 TFLOPS)** |
| down (`m=4096, k=2048`) | 6.40 ms (0.50 TFLOPS) | **30.02 ms (1.72 TFLOPS)** |

Evidence: `results/2026/09/b70-improvement-probes-20260920/perf-k216-prefill-n512.txt`.

Reading it against the model-level prefill measurement:

- Per layer the routed projections cost 2 × 28.28 + 30.02 = **86.6 ms** at ub 512; across 43 layers
  that is **3.72 s** for one 512-token ubatch, against a measured **5.06 s** (101.2 pp) with 15 of
  those layers' experts on CPU. The routed MoE path therefore accounts for essentially the whole
  prefill budget — there is no large unattributed remainder to hunt.
- **The gap that matters is efficiency, not orchestration count:** the same operator reaches
  1.72–1.82 TFLOPS at n=512, while a comparable dense `MUL_MAT` on this card reaches ~45 TFLOPS
  (`q4_0`, m=4096, n=512, k=14336, same harness). That is roughly a **25× gap** — and weight bytes
  favour the IQ3 path (5× smaller than FP16), so the gap is the IQ3→FP16 materialization plus the
  per-expert launch structure, exactly what the phase's device-side grouping + tile-local
  reconstruction targets.

**Attribution verdict: P3's premise holds with numbers.** The grouping/reconstruction/launch path
is not a hidden fraction of prefill — it is the dominant cost of every routed projection, and the
operator is two dozen times less efficient than the card's dense GEMM at the same ubatch. This also
gives the phase its success metric: any grouped-IQ3 kernel that does not materialize whole experts
should move 1.8 TFLOPS toward the dense class, which is where the 1.5–2.0× prefill E0 would come
from. The phase's kill criterion (withdraw if grouping+expansion < ~30 % of prefill) is **not
triggered**.

## The ubatch lever is exhausted at 1024 — measured, not extrapolated

Extended the sweep past the recorded 512→1024 pair (`scripts/tmp/p3-prefill-ub-20260921.sh`,
`UBS="2048 4096"`, same cell and placement, n=3 per config):

| `-b`/`-ub` | engine pp (median of 3) | vs ub 1024 |
|---|---:|---:|
| 512 | 101.20 | — |
| 1024 | 130.78 | **1.292×** |
| **2048** | **131.10** (124.11 / 131.10 / 131.21) | **+0.2 %** |
| 4096 | **crashed** — `level_zero backend failed with error: 39 (UR_RESULT_ERROR_OUT_OF_DEVICE_MEMORY)` | — |

Three things follow, and the first two are corrections:

1. **The curve saturates at 1024.** An architectural extrapolation had predicted +17 % at 2048 and
   +28 % at 4096 from a two-point fit; the measurement says **+0.2 %** at 2048 and that 4096 does not
   even load at this placement. Two points do not make a curve, and this is what it looks like when a
   config lever is already spent.
2. **4096 is out of device memory at this configuration** (`-c 8448`, dual card, `-ncmoe 15`,
   `-ts 65,35`), so it is not a configuration to reach for — it would need a placement/context change,
   which would confound any attribution.
3. **The prefill lever that remains is the kernel.** With the ubatch path exhausted, P3's entire
   opportunity is the grouped/tile-local reconstruction operator, whose measured baseline is
   recaptured in `results/2026/09/b70-improvement-probes-20260920/perf-k216-prefill-n512.txt`
   (gate/up 28.87 ms/op ≈ 1.79 TFLOPS at n=512) — that file is the ≥2× gate's reference, and it is no
   longer empty.
