# P6 worklog — entry probe, 2026-09-21

Phase: P6 (long-context and mixed-load correctness gates), first work item: classify the
`CL_OUT_OF_RESOURCES` failure at ≥196K that was filed upstream as openvino.genai #4483. The phase's
framing is that this is *probably misclassified* — "CL_OUT_OF_RESOURCES ≠ VRAM OOM" — and that the
cheap win is to fix the classification before anyone reaches for capacity changes.

Harness: `scripts/tmp/p6-ov-oor-probe-20260921.sh` (built and reviewed by a separate agent, run here).
Plan/review notes: `worklogs/P6-oor-classification-plan-20260921.md`.

## Result — the failure reproduces, and it is NOT a misclassification

| attempt | context | protocol | result |
|---|---:|---|---|
| light control | 196,608 | 40 tokens | PASS |
| full | 131,072 | full protocol | PASS |
| full | 163,840 | full protocol (111 tokens, TTFT 395 s, 14.34 tps) | PASS |
| full | **196,608** | full protocol | **FAIL, exit 134: `[GPU] clFinish, error code: -5 CL_OUT_OF_RESOURCES`** |

Per-stage VRAM, from the probe's own samples (`epoch_s, usage_bytes, visible_avail_mib,
max_free_order_mib, pkg_c, power_uw`):

| moment | usage | free | largest free block |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| steady state at 196,608 before the failure | 28.94 GB | 5,052 MiB | 2,048 MiB |
| **at the failure** | **34.20 GB** | **44 MiB** | **16 MiB** |

**So the device really does run out.** At the moment of failure the card had 44 MiB free and its
largest contiguous block was 16 MiB; usage had *grown* from 28.9 GB to 34.2 GB during the run. The
failure coincides with memory exhaustion — it is not a workgroup/private-memory limit hit with
plenty of VRAM free, which is the hypothesis the phase's framing prefers.

Two further facts from the same run:
1. **The light 40-token probe PASSES at the same 196,608 context** where the full protocol fails ⇒
   the trigger is generation length, not the static context allocation.
2. **The boundary for this configuration is between 163,840 and 196,608** (both full-protocol
   endpoints measured).

## The one thing the probe could not deliver, and why

Its predeclared rule returns `VERDICT=UNDETERMINED (R3_no_decisive_evidence)`. Cause: the attempt's
first action, `PROP_SET log.level`, **failed** — the runtime answered
`Option not found: LOG_LEVEL` — so the OV DEBUG log that would have shown *which* allocation failed
was never captured. The VRAM samples above are decisive on their own about *what happened*
(exhaustion) but not about *what grew*.

**Consequence for the phase's plan:** the entry assumption is refuted. The failure at ≥196K in this
configuration is a genuine capacity failure, so the honest next steps are capacity-shaped (KV/precision
or buffer sizing, or declaring the supported context below 196K) — not a kernel-configuration hunt.
A single cheap follow-up would attribute the growth: re-run only the 196,608 full attempt with a
correct log-level property (and optionally `NEO_CACHE_PERSISTENT=0` plus a per-stage allocation dump).

Also recorded: the cooldown gate (≤60 °C) timed out at 70–71 °C before two attempts (the probe
proceeds and says so); the card's pkg temperature was 70–80 °C through the long attempts.

## Follow-up — the threshold is not within one generation

The classification probe's own modes were 40 tokens ("light", a single generation — **passes** at
196608) and 128 tokens ("full", a *warmup plus* measured generation — **fails**), so its two data
points differ in two things at once: generation length *and* the number of generations per process.
The ladder separates them (`scripts/tmp/p6-ov-ladder-run-20260921.sh` +
`p6-ov-token-ladder.py`, one process, one generation per attempt unless `P6_REPS` says otherwise,
196608 context, u4 KV):

| attempt | generations in the process | result | wall | decode |
|---|---:|---|---:|---:|
| 40 tokens (probe's light mode) | 1 | **PASS** | — | — |
| 64 tokens | 1 | **PASS** | 550.8 s (TTFT 545.8 s) | 12.78 tps |
| 96 tokens | 1 | **PASS** | 553.4 s (TTFT 546.1 s) | 13.11 tps |
| 128 + 128 tokens (probe's full mode) | 2 | **FAIL** | — | — |

**So the failure is not reached by a single long generation at this context** — 96 tokens of
generation at 196,608 passed while the two-generation 128+128 protocol failed — and the VRAM stayed
at its steady state (28.94 GB used, 5,052 MiB free) through the single-generation attempts rather
than climbing toward the 34.20 GB seen at the failure. That points at **growth per generation
(per request) rather than per token**, which is a leak-shaped signature and a much more specific
statement than "it runs out of memory".

`P6_REPS=2` at 64 tokens is the test that decides it: if two 64-token generations fail where one
96-token generation passed, the trigger is the *number of long generations in one process*, and the
honest next action is an upstream report with this evidence rather than a capacity hunt.

## Decisive result — it is the SECOND long generation, at any length

`P6_REPS=2` at 64 tokens, 196,608 context, u4 KV, one process:

| generation in the process | result |
|---|---|
| #1 (64 tokens) | **PASS** — wall 551.0 s, TTFT 546.1 s, 12.78 tps |
| #2 (64 tokens) | **FAIL** — `RuntimeError … ocl_stream.cpp:395: [GPU] clFinish, error code: -5 CL_OUT_OF_RESOURCES`, exit 134 |

Combined with the ladder (a *single* generation of 96 tokens passes where two of 128 fail), the
classification is now specific and reproducible:

> **At 196,608 context with u4 KV, one long generation succeeds; the second long generation in the
> same process fails with `CL_OUT_OF_RESOURCES`, independent of the generation length (64+64 fails,
> 96 alone passes).** The card is driven to ~34.2 GB with 44 MiB free at the failure, having held a
> steady 28.94 GB through the single-generation runs.

That is a **per-generation growth / not-returned-allocation** signature in the ≥196 K path, not a
capacity ceiling and not a workgroup/private-memory misclassification. It also explains the recorded
campaign's failure: its "full protocol" is a *warmup plus* a measured generation — two long
generations in one process — so the ≥196 K full-protocol failure was this bug, while the same
context is fine for a single long generation.

**Upstream-report shape for openvino.genai #4483 (evidence in this directory):** two 64-token
generations at 196,608 with `KV_CACHE_PRECISION='u4'` on Arc Pro B70; first passes, second fails with
`CL_OUT_OF_RESOURCES` at 44 MiB free / 16 MiB largest block; 96 tokens in one generation passes;
131,072 and 163,840 full protocols pass. Runtime: openvino 2026.5.0-22967-38a575de45f, openvino-genai
2026.5.0.0-3412-8fa12145144.
