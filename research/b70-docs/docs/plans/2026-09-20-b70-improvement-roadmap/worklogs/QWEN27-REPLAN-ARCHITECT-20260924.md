# Qwen3.8-27B C1 replan — diagnostic before engineering

**PROVISIONAL — NOT FOR PUBLIC HEADLINE.** Research-only architectural decision, not a new benchmark or authorization to occupy a GPU. Scope is **Qwen3.8-27B alone** on the existing single-card vLLM XPU target: GPTQ INT4 G128 body, graph capture, MTP4, draft-side INT4 head/body, unquantized BF16/FP16 full-vocabulary target `lm_head`, stock MTP weights. No retraining, target requantization, engine migration, TP2 campaign, server action, or build is approved by this note. The separate upstream review is `~/B70-DOCS/docs/plans/2026-09-20-b70-improvement-roadmap/worklogs/QWEN27-REPLAN-UPSTREAM-20260924.md`; independent architect review remains owed.

## Decision

**Only first commitment: one bounded, exact-champion-stack C1 step-attribution diagnostic, handed to the bench/profiler owner.** Do not commission a target GEMM, head, graph, GDN, rotated-quantization, or collective implementation until this diagnostic identifies an *exclusive critical-path* opportunity with a conservative **>=5% net end-to-end C1 post-first throughput** bound at unchanged output semantics and acceptable validation cost. The old profiler cannot decide that on the retained build. A small Level Zero bidirectional peer-capability discriminator is **second and parked**, not a TP2 project. Independent stacks remain the two-card default.

## Yield, timing, and byte-model corrections

For k=4 drafts let `s_j = P(the first j proposals all pass)` for j=1..4. The expected number of emissions per full verification round is `Y = 1 + s_1 + s_2 + s_3 + s_4`. When a *single measured window* supplies cumulative accepted drafts `A` and completed draft rounds `R`, the proxy `Y ≈ 1 + A/R = 1 + k*alpha` where `alpha = A/(kR)`. The recorded positional ratios are **marginal survival rates, each with the same round denominator**, not four independent conditional probabilities to add. The proxy assumes round and bonus-token accounting are consistent; independently measure actual emitted tokens and engine steps before presenting an exact engine-step yield.

- Same-day stock MTP baseline, C1 p512/g128 recommended non-thinking sampling: 438 accepted / 202 drafts (808 proposals), alpha 0.5421, Y proxy **3.1683**; positional marginal survival `0.9802, 0.9752, 0.1238, 0.0891`; client post-first median **73.68 tok/s**, n=5. Raw: `~/B70-DOCS/results/2026/09/b70-improvement-probes-20260920/d11-baseline-decay-probe-20260924T142540Z/decay_curve.json`.
- D11 FastMTP-1500 retrained arm, same-day matched: 430 / 214 (856 proposals), alpha 0.5023, Y proxy **3.0093**; positional marginal survival `0.8318, 0.6869, 0.2804, 0.2103`; client post-first median **71.83 tok/s**, n=5. The larger deep-position terms cannot compensate for early survival collapse; measured throughput **-2.5%** versus the same-day baseline (not the different Run-48 build/window). Raw: `~/B70-DOCS/results/2026/09/b70-improvement-probes-20260920/d11-stitched-fastmtp1500-probe-20260924T172339Z/stitched_results.json`. The `T182834Z` result-root suffix cited in OUTCOMES L15 and LEVERS D11 was **not present** in this static review; the shared-file owner should correct the evidence link. We did not edit those authorities.
- At fixed baseline yield, **hypothetical** 40 ms/round predicts `3.1683 / .040 = 79.2 tok/s`; 25 ms predicts **126.7 tok/s**; 140 tok/s needs **<=22.63 ms/round**. At 40 ms, 140 would need 5.6 emissions/round, exceeding the k=4 maximum of 5. Dividing the window's counter-derived 3.1683 by its **median** client 73.68 tok/s gives about 43 ms as an *order-of-magnitude implied budget only*: aggregate counters, client median, per-request boundaries and non-decode time do not form one exact observed engine-round measurement. The 39.8 ms step quoted in the EXL3 note belongs to a differently derived budget/build and cannot silently be substituted for D11 or Run 48.
- `~/B70-DOCS/research/2026/exl3-analysis-and-next-inference-levers-20260924.md` §4 lists 15.20 GB target body + 2.54 GB unchanged verifier head + 3.40 GB four draft passes + 0.30 GB KV/state ≈21.44 GB *nominal* bytes/round and divides by a separately cited 39.8 ms to derive ~539 GB/s. This is **not a measured off-chip counter trace** of the champion; weights may be reused in cache or reread, dequant/GDN and launch costs may overlap, and the external EXL3 screenshot has no primary provenance in the upstream review. The note's `21.44/560≈38.3 ms` floor depends on an unverified practical peak and exact streaming assumption. Its `13.1 GB/540≈24.3 ms` rotated/EXL3 arithmetic counts a **different main-weight artifact alone**, omits draft/head/state, and borrows EXL3's 3.42 yield. Do not call 25 ms or 140 tok/s a champion prediction.

## One exact first-probe handoff (bench/profiler owner, NOT run here)

**Purpose:** discriminate whether this retained C1 route has >=5% *net* addressable decode time in a named target W4A16/small-M, full-precision head, repeated draft, GDN, or graph/launch component. **Hard wall-time <=60 minutes total**, including readiness, control, instrumentation and cleanup; any startup, model-load, graph-capture or compile failure ends the diagnostic within that deadline, without expanding to a build/sweep. One owner, one card, one server, one client. No modifications to model/weights or cap as a variable.

1. Owner runs normal empty-GPU/process/container/VRAM and dynamically resolved hwmon/power-cap gates in `AGENTS.md` §§3–5, chooses a free card, pins exact immutable champion image digest plus patch/overlay identity, checkpoint hash, kernel wheel, graph-enabled log, MTP4, both draft-side INT4 toggles, unchanged target verify head, fp8 KV, recommended non-thinking sampling, cap/cooldown, `max-num-seqs=1`. If either card/resource is occupied by another owner, **stop and escalate**. Keep boot spec-canary as a correctness gate. Never switch to eager to make a trace convenient.
2. Use calibrated C1 p512/g128 as the sole diagnostic shape (actual endpoint tokens logged, all 128 completions with `finish_reason=length`), unique cold-prefix entropy, cache-counter snapshots requiring zero hits, exactly one client, a discarded same-shape warmup. Take an **uninstrumented control before**, one instrumented replay window, and an **uninstrumented control after**, preserving raw per-request client post-first rates, actual token/round and acceptance counters, temperature/cap, graph mode and all failures. A p8192/g128 n=3 follow-up is separately gated if and only if the first probe establishes a credible target component; do not expand the initial probe to a matrix.
3. Capture evidence that instrumented executions are **the same graph-replay path** as controls (and dispatch/shape attribution is visible). Label target GPTQ W4A16 verification GEMMs by layer and actual M/K/N shape; distinguish the unchanged-precision full-vocabulary verifier `lm_head` from the **four** quantized draft heads and MTP bodies, GDN and attention/KV state work, copies, graph launch/CPU wait and remaining overhead. Align GPU events with round boundaries and actual emitted tokens. Attribute **exclusive critical-path round time**, overlap, and the residual; **never sum overlapping kernel durations into a step budget**. Off-chip byte counters are optional and only claim bandwidth if correctly phase-aligned and validated; do not divide nominal model bytes by mixed-window client latency.
4. Compare the two uninstrumented controls for drift and the instrumented window for materially changed shape, graph dispatch, acceptance, temperature, cap, prefix hits or completions. If graph replay is opaque in this tool, instrumentation changes dispatch, isolation fails, or exclusive shares cannot be bounded, report **`headroom_unestablished`** with the missing observable, and stop; do not substitute an eager trace or launch a new profiler campaign automatically.
5. Report conservative before/after-control C1 metric coordinates, acceptance, step/yield accounting, each candidate's *exclusive* time fraction `f`, realistically removable fraction `r`, expected extra overhead `h`, and `S_net <= T / (T - f*r*T + h)` at **unchanged yield**. At minimum `1-1/1.05 = 4.762%` of round time must be saved for +5% throughput; with a 2x-faster subcomponent (`r=0.5`) it must occupy **>=9.524%** exclusive round time before integration overhead. This is an Amdahl bound, not a performance claim. If no component meets a conservative >=5% net C1 bound and output-parity/correctness gate, **close new engineering** on this stack and return a measured no-go, not another sweep.

## Conditional branches after the diagnostic (no implementation authorization yet)

1. **Target GPTQ dequant / M≈5 reuse** only if exact target dispatch and exclusive fraction clear the >=5% gate. Mechanism: reduce redundant W4A16 scale/unpack and underfilled small-M matrix work while maintaining byte layout and target logits. Contends with XVE instructions, DPAS tile utilization, XVE's 64 KB register file, per-core SLM and GDDR traffic; beware adding enough registers/SLM to lower occupancy. Upper bound: measured exclusive `f` if eliminated entirely, or `f/2` time saved for a 2x-local improvement, minus fusion/capture overhead; no transferable K216 MoE speed multiplier. Cheapest falsifier: exact-stack traced target-only M≈5 dispatch plus bounded isolated-op vs same-shape reference and target-logit parity, then matched C1 A/B only if the isolated gate predicts >=5% net.
2. **Unquantized target `lm_head` at identical precision and full vocabulary** only if its measured exclusive time independently passes the same gate. Mechanism: layout/tile/weight reuse without changing logits, verification, vocabulary, or the drafted head. Contends with DRAM bandwidth/cache, DPAS utilization, registers/SLM; preserving BF16 bytes and full-vocab semantics imposes a nonzero read floor under a streaming assumption. Exact net ceiling is the observed *exclusive* head fraction, not automatically the nominal 2.54 GB/assumed bandwidth. Cheapest falsifier: head dispatch/critical-path observation plus unchanged full-logit numerical comparison. Direct INT4 and FP8 target head **fail prior top-1 quality gates** (OUTCOMES L10; EXL3 note §5); candidate-only slicing needs exact normalization and full-vocab fallback and is not a preservation shortcut.
3. **Repeated MTP/graph-launch residual or hybrid-GDN** only if a named exclusive fraction clears >=5% after capture. Mechanism: graph-safe fusion or reuse to remove true serialized work; contends with scheduling, register pressure, state/KV traffic and correctness under mixed speculative/conventional GDN paths. Upper bound is the *new* graph-on exclusive region, not the old eager GDN totals or an assumed 500 us host-launch penalty. Cheapest falsifier: phase-aligned graph replay critical path and spec-canary/counter guard before any patch. No speculative depth retune, probabilistic draft, bf16 draft-KV or light D11 head retraining without an actually changed, approved mechanism: they failed matched gates (`LEVERS.md` D9–D14).
4. **Cold prefill/TTFT** is a different objective and separate p2048/g1 and p8192/g1 cold-prefix/counter-gated lane. No dense GPTQ prefill share has been shown that could import K216's IQ3 MoE `MUL_MAT_ID` speedup. Even a hypothetical 3.60x speedup needs prefill-wall fraction >=46.2% for 1.5x e2e or >=69.2% for 2.0x (Amdahl, zero integration overhead); that routed llama.cpp IQ3 kernel uses a different format, operation and engine. Source: `OUTCOMES.md` W14/L9/scope retirement and `LEVERS.md` P-3b. Concurrency gains remain Cn-only.

## Three proposed priorities: decision and falsifiers

- **QuaRot / INT3/INT4 +20–30%, 39.8→25 ms, 140 tok/s: REJECT as a commitment on this unchanged target.** Orthogonal rotation changes quantization/activation representation and needs a new artifact, hybrid-GDN applicability, correctness/quality evidence and supported fast dispatch; INT4 rotation by itself removes no nominal bytes. Even an ideal INT3 packing of the cited 15.20 GB four-bit body saves only ≈3.80 GB, ≈7.0 ms at an *assumed* 540 GB/s, before increased unpack/rotation, unchanged head, four draft passes and state. On Xe2 sub-8-bit rate-class effects are operand-dependent and W4A16 is not native A4; see `~/B70-DOCS/research/2026/xmx-dpas-rate-classes-20260915.md`. This estimate cannot justify 25 ms; cheapest falsifier under the **current-target** scope is the first-probe measured target body/head fraction and byte-traffic bounds, not conversion. A future separate artifact/quality decision would need separate authorization.
- **IQ3_XXS 3.60x MoE op into dense GPTQ for 1.5–2x prefill: INAPPLICABLE.** `#29245` targets grouped llama.cpp SYCL `MUL_MAT_ID` on GGUF IQ formats, not dense vLLM XPU GPTQ. K216 isolated-op acceleration was real but its end-to-end prefill rung was pending and K216 work retired. Falsifier would require identifying a same-format same-op dense dispatch—which static source review in `~/B70-DOCS/docs/plans/2026-09-20-b70-improvement-roadmap/worklogs/QWEN27-REPLAN-UPSTREAM-20260924.md` §2.1 rules out.
- **Level Zero IPC zero-copy TP2 >1 TB/s and +50–70% C1: NO SPEED COMMITMENT, local peer capability UNKNOWN.** Level Zero cross-device IPC requires *existing* P2P; it cannot create it. Pooled >1 TB/s can accurately describe the **sum of two cards' local GDDR streams** under ideal sharding, **not the PCIe transfer path**. The current X570-F topology note establishes dual CPU-attached x8/x8 and H2D probe-limited floors but gives **no local `zeDeviceCanAccessPeer` query or checksum-tested D2D copy**. Intel compute-runtime #935 reports corruption/refusal on some Intel roots but working bidirectional P2P on other AMD hosts; do not transplant either result onto this host. Even a capability PASS would not establish graph-safe oneCCL, C1 scaling or compatibility with the TP>1 draft-INT4 implementation (which fails closed there). The externally reported host-staged TP2 ratio is different artifacts/protocols, not a prediction for this champion. Default remains independent stacks per `~/B70-DOCS/decisions/adr-0008-dual-b70-independent-default.md`.

**Parked second discriminator (owned bench engineer, separate empty-both-card window, ~1–2 minutes after setup, no model):** identify BDFs and driver; query `zeDeviceCanAccessPeer` both directions and `zeDeviceGetP2PProperties` ACCESS/ATOMICS flags; execute bounded dev0→dev1 and dev1→dev0 copies (e.g. 256 MiB, initialized with distinguishable patterns) and byte-for-byte destination checksum, with a host-staged copy control. Do **not** change PCI whitelist, ACS, sysfs power/routing or driver settings to force success. Peer denied, copy error, or checksum mismatch closes *direct-peer* H4 on this host; clean PASS establishes transport capability **only**, then a *separately authorized* oneCCL/graph-safety screen is needed before any TP2 campaign. Existing no-P2P assertions in the topology note/ADR are a plausible operational default, not a measured local capability verdict. The primary source and reproducer are linked in `~/B70-DOCS/docs/plans/2026-09-20-b70-improvement-roadmap/worklogs/QWEN27-REPLAN-UPSTREAM-20260924.md` §1.
