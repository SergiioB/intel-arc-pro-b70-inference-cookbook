## 1. Executive verdict

The highest-value sequence is:

1. **Recover and identify the B70 correctly before benchmarking.** The current IP answers ping and TCP/22, but does not send an SSH banner. Its observed MAC differs from the known WoL Ethernet MAC. This can be a second interface on the same workstation or an IP conflict; it must be mapped, not guessed.
2. **Preserve b9853 and use it as the rollback/correctness baseline.** Do not replace its binary, build directory or service profile.
3. **Build current llama.cpp master separately, but treat its default DMMV change as a correctness blocker.** Master is 158 commits ahead of b9853. A 2026-07-14 B70 report bisected Qwen3.6-35B-A3B Q4_K second-turn corruption to #25063 (`K_QUANTS_PER_ITERATION=1`); rebuilding with `K_QUANTS_PER_ITERATION=2` reportedly fixed it. Compare b9853, current default and KQPI=2 before accepting any master result.
4. **Test Vulkan as a correctness and performance control, not as a replacement by assumption.** Community B70 results show Vulkan can have much faster prefill, while SYCL often has better decode. Vulkan also gained native MXFP4/NVFP4 conversion support on 2026-07-13.
5. **Prioritize the experimental XMX long-context attention path.** PR #25312 reports approximately 2.48x prefill on B70 with quantized KV. Direct inspection of its current code shows independently converted K/V types and no q4_1 exclusion, so `q8_0` K / `q4_1` V is hybrid-eligible when all shape/runtime gates pass. This exact pair has not been upstream-benchmarked or quality-validated, so it must still be compared with q8/q8 and q8/q5_0.
6. **Test MXFP4 before NVFP4.** Start with native MXFP4 gpt-oss-20B, then Qwen3.6-35B-A3B MXFP4_MOE. NVFP4_MOE GGUF can run through llama.cpp on Intel, but B70 has no Blackwell-native FP4 tensor path.
7. **Add Ornith-1.0-35B Q4_K_M as the first new coding-agent model challenger.** Its developer-reported SWE-bench Verified 75.6, SWE-bench Pro 50.4 and Terminal-Bench 2.1 64.2 make it the most relevant new one-card candidate. It fits at about 19.71 GiB, but must beat the existing Qwen models on the local patch/test/tool harness.
8. **Keep Qwen3.6-27B dense + MTP as the proven local quality route.** Also investigate the highly customized Qwen27 AutoRound INT4/ReplaySSM recipe that reported 68.236 strict/fresh tok/s on one B70, but do not confuse it with stock vLLM reproducibility.
9. **Promote OpenVINO/OVMS to the highest-confidence alternate-runtime experiment.** OpenVINO 2026.2 explicitly documents Qwen3.6-35B-A3B INT4 tested on a B70, with reasoning, tools and vision. It publishes no throughput number, so reproduction is still required.
10. **Treat llm-scaler/vLLM 0.21 as an isolated throughput experiment and defer SGLang.** The 20.914 GB Qwen35 AutoRound artifact publishes TP=1 intent, while Intel's FP8/SGLang Qwen35 recipes are still TP=2. No result is promotable without quality, backend-op and multi-turn gates.

| Grade | Meaning | How it is used |
|---|---|---|
| A | Reproduced on this exact B70 | Can guide defaults, subject to rerun after recovery |
| B | Upstream or Intel result measured on a B70 | High-priority experiment, not a local fact |
| C | Community B70/B60 result with commands/configuration | Directional; reproduce locally |
| D | Different GPU/runtime or model-card claim | Hypothesis/model-selection signal only |

Rules:

- Do not compare a parallel/shared-prefix number to single-request decode.
- Do not compare mixed prefill+decode wall throughput to pure token generation.
- Do not transfer CUDA/Blackwell MXFP4 or NVFP4 claims directly to SYCL.
- Do not equate successful model loading with an optimized kernel path.
- Do not treat vendor model benchmark scores as quantized-checkpoint quality scores unless the quant itself was evaluated.

### 4.3 SSH-banner diagnostics

A TCP accept followed by no SSH banner can be high load, a hung sshd/PAM/NSS path, exhausted file descriptors, `MaxStartups`, or the wrong host.

```bash
sudo systemctl status ssh --no-pager
sudo journalctl -u ssh -b --no-pager
sudo ss -tnp '( sport = :22 )'
sudo sshd -T | grep -Ei 'maxstartups|maxsessions|usedns|gssapiauthentication'
cat /proc/loadavg
ulimit -n
systemctl show ssh -p NFileDescriptorStore -p TasksCurrent -p MemoryCurrent
getent passwd "$USER"
getent hosts "$(hostname)"
```

Do not blindly change SSH timeouts until the blocking component is identified.

### 4.4 Recovery hardening

1. Disable unattended suspend until three controlled suspend/WoL/SSH/inference cycles pass.
2. Make the wake monitor distinguish:
   - packet sent,
   - expected MAC present,
   - ICMP,
   - TCP/22,
   - SSH banner,
   - authenticated command,
   - inference health.
3. Use a minimum five-minute post-WoL monitor.
4. Add a smart plug with BIOS “restore on AC power” as the practical out-of-band reset path.
5. Keep router WoL and a second LAN machine as independent wake paths.
6. Stop inference before suspend and verify the GPU/xe driver has quiesced.
7. Never start unattended experimental sweeps until recovery works reliably.

### 7.3 Experimental long-context Flash Attention

#### #25025: oneMKL GEMM attention

- Open, currently blocked in merge state.
- Uses XMX through oneMKL for prefill with Q >= 32 and K >= 1024.
- Supports all KV types by dequantizing where needed, including the current `q4_1` V.
- Upstream B70 results are around 1.9x for Q8 quantized-KV prefill in the later hybrid comparison.
- Main role: broad fallback and current `q8/q4_1` candidate.

#### #25222: oneDNN SDPA for F16 KV

- Open and blocked.
- F16 KV only.
- B70 report: 1.21x at 512-token prompt and up to 4.26x near 80K.
- Excellent architecture proof, but F16 KV is usually too large for a 27B model at 128K on a 32 GB card.

#### #25312: oneDNN hybrid for quantized KV

- Open and currently dirty against master.
- Highest potential for the actual 32 GB long-context workload.
- Reported B70 Qwen 27B Q8 KV prefill: approximately 332 → 824 tok/s at a 32K prompt/deep context, about 2.48x.
- Reported Gemma 31B Q8 KV: approximately 178 → 440 tok/s, about 2.48x.
- Decode stayed near baseline in the submitted tests.

Important compatibility detail:

- The PR description's dispatch table omits q4_1, but the current hybrid support function does not implement that whitelist.
- It rejects only native F16/F16 before checking masks, dimensions, sinks, bias/softcap and sequence shape.
- K and V are independently converted through `ggml_get_to_fp16_sycl(type)`, and the inherited type dispatch contains `GGML_TYPE_Q4_1`.
- Therefore `q8_0` K / `q4_1` V is **code-path eligible** for hybrid priority when oneDNN/FA is enabled, query batch >=32, KV length >=1024, the mask is F16, and unsupported sinks/bias/softcap/multi-sequence cases are absent.
- There is no submitted B70 q8/q4_1 benchmark or quality result. Confirm path selection by comparing `GGML_SYCL_FA_ONEDNN=1` versus `=0` while leaving MKL enabled.

## 9. Backend strategy and community evidence

### SYCL

Strengths:

- Best-known local decode path.
- Fused MoE TG, reorder kernels and dense MTP support.
- Current upstream focus is active.

Risks:

- Current MoE prefill correctness issue.
- Flash Attention prefill decays badly with context on the old XVE path.
- Environment variables changed after b9853.
- Driver/kernel/oneAPI combinations can trigger xe BCS faults and hangs.

### Vulkan

Community B70 examples:

- Qwen3.6-35B-A3B Q4_K_M: about 1344 pp512 and 44.12 tg128.
- Gemma 4 26B-A4B Q4_K_M: about 1736 pp512 and 41.62 tg128.
- Qwen3.6-27B Q4_K_M at 55K prompt: about 495.6 pp and 15.97 tg.

Directional conclusion:

- Vulkan can deliver much faster prefill than old SYCL while sometimes losing decode.
- It is valuable as a correctness control for MoE and FP4.
- It must be built and measured on the same Linux system; Windows Vulkan reports cannot be used as the local baseline.

### llm-scaler/vLLM

Latest release:

- `intel/llm-scaler-vllm:0.21.0-b1`, published 2026-07-10.
- vLLM 0.21.0.
- Experimental XPU graph.
- Gemma 4 12B/31B/26B-A4B and diffusiongemma support.
- No official one-B70 end-to-end throughput result was published with this release.
- Intel's Qwen3.6-35B-A3B AutoRound artifact is 20,914,474,115 bytes and publishes a TP=1 vLLM recipe; this proves artifact/recipe intent, not validation on this exact image/B70.

Community reality:

- Qwen 27B dense FP8 on one B70 has been reported around 13–14 tok/s single request and unstable when context/memory were pushed.
- High-concurrency runs can report hundreds of output tok/s, but with very high TTFT and dozens of concurrent requests.
- Older releases produced working chat while reasoning separation/tool calling failed.
- FP8 KV without calibrated scales has explicit accuracy warnings.
- XPU graph had a concrete ESIMD MoE stale-buffer defect that produced NaNs/`!!!!` after graph/eager batch-size transitions. PR #505 merged on 2026-07-01, but the 0.21.0-b1 tag is on a diverged release line; verify the fix or equivalent code exists in the actual image before enabling graph.

Use case:

- Candidate for concurrency and Intel INT4/FP8 kernels.
- Not the first single-stream daily-driver candidate.

### SGLang

Intel merged initial BMG support on 2026-07-14. The published recipe:

- Qwen3.6-35B-A3B.
- Online FP8.
- TP=2.
- ESIMD decode, MoE, GDN and DPAS prefill kernels.
- XPU graph.

It is not currently a one-B70 experiment. Monitor it for a TP=1/layered-load recipe before spending time on integration.

### OpenVINO

- OpenVINO 2026.2 officially enables Qwen3.5/3.6, Qwen3-Coder-Next and Gemma 4 on Intel GPUs, plus GPU INT4 KV-cache compression for long prompts.
- The OVMS agent recipe explicitly states that `OpenVINO/Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-int4-ov` was tested on a B70 dGPU and demonstrates reasoning parsing, Qwen tool parsing and vision/MCP use.
- This is the strongest official one-B70 functionality claim found, but Intel publishes no tok/s, TTFT, TPOT or long-context quality result for it.
- Gemma 4 MoE is supported without continuous batching in OVMS 2026.2, limiting its serving-throughput comparison.
- Promote OpenVINO to the first alternate-runtime reproduction, ahead of vLLM, while still measuring resident VRAM, kernel path and task quality.

- SGLang Qwen3.6 official BMG recipe: currently TP=2.

## 11. Weight-format plan

### Q/K quants

- Do not assume the nominally smaller quant is faster.
- Existing B70/B60 evidence shows kernel layout dominates: Q4_K/Q4_0 can greatly outperform IQ quants.
- LocalMaxxing single-B70 community entries show Qwen 27B Q4_0 around 28.8 tok/s versus Q4_K_S around 26.7 tok/s in those specific runs.
- Current upstream has Q5_K correctness reports on SYCL MoE. Keep Q5 as a quality reference only until operation and multi-turn gates pass.

Required Qwen 35B matrix:

1. Q4_0
2. Q4_K_M or Q4_K_XL
3. Q5_K_M/Q5_K_XL only after correctness gate
4. Q8_0 as a quality/kernel reference if it fits
5. MXFP4_MOE
6. NVFP4_MOE

### MXFP4

Why first:

- OCP microscaling format.
- Supported by llama.cpp SYCL MoE kernels.
- gpt-oss-20B was post-trained and evaluated with native MXFP4 expert weights.
- Provides a cleaner implementation control than post-quantizing Qwen immediately.
- Qwen35 MXFP4_MOE is 21,706,144,736 bytes versus 22,134,528,992 bytes for UD-Q4_K_M: only about 0.40 GiB saved.
- Gemma4-26B-A4B MXFP4_MOE is 16,551,046,944 bytes versus 16,947,539,744 bytes for UD-Q4_K_M: only about 0.37 GiB saved.
- Therefore these FP4 GGUFs are kernel/quality experiments, not context-capacity breakthroughs.

Limit:

- B70 does not have Blackwell-native FP4 tensor cores.
- The gain, if any, comes from expert-weight bandwidth and fused decode, not NVIDIA FP4 instructions.

### NVFP4

- A Blackwell compressed-tensors checkpoint is not automatically portable.
- An NVFP4_MOE GGUF can be decoded by llama.cpp on Intel if the tensors/model architecture are supported.
- Compare loading, selected kernel path, decode, prefill, VRAM and quality.
- Upstream cross-format discussion showed NVFP4 can have materially worse KLD than Q4_K despite good speed; do not judge it by size/tok/s alone.

### AutoRound INT4

- Intel publishes Qwen3.6-35B-A3B and Gemma 4 26B-A4B W4A16 models.
- Packing format matters. Older llm-scaler versions accepted only selected GPTQ/compressed-tensors paths.
- Current Intel ESIMD kernel microbenchmarks report INT4/FP8 MoE ratios from roughly parity to 1.36x depending on batch.
- This is not proof of end-to-end single-card gain.

## 12. KV-cache experiment

Current provisional default: `q8_0` K / `q4_1` V.

Rationale:

- K errors directly perturb attention scores and are usually more sensitive.
- V can often tolerate stronger compression.
- The asymmetric configuration saves substantial VRAM while protecting K.

Required matrix:

| ID | K | V | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| KV0 | f16 | f16 | Reference where it fits |
| KV1 | q8_0 | q8_0 | Quantized quality/fast-hybrid reference |
| KV2 | q8_0 | q5_0 | #25312 hybrid-compatible candidate |
| KV3 | q8_0 | q4_1 | Current capacity/quality compromise; #25312 code is hybrid-eligible but this pair lacks an upstream B70 benchmark |
| KV4 | q5_0 | q4_1 | Extra capacity candidate |
| KV5 | q4_0 | q4_0 | Stress point only |

Depths:

- 0/2K
- 8K
- 32K
- 64K
- 128K

Do not choose by perplexity alone. Measure:

- Mean and p95 next-token KL.
- Top-1 token agreement.
- Long-context retrieval.
- Multi-turn consistency.
- Structured output/tool success.
- Decode and prefill separately.
- VRAM and maximum stable concurrency.

## 13. Benchmark execution protocol

### 13.1 Safety preflight

```bash
pgrep -af 'llama-(bench|server)' && { echo 'Another inference process is running'; exit 1; }
source /opt/intel/oneapi/setvars.sh
```

The local stack has previously shown `llama-bench` printing valid results but hanging on cleanup. Capture output, detect the completed TG row and terminate only after results are flushed. Never run two sweeps in parallel.

### 13.2 Standard single-build performance loop

Smoke-test `--help` on every new binary first. `llama-bench` does not support server-only options such as `--ctx-size` or `--parallel`.

```bash
BIN=/path/to/build/bin/llama-bench
MODEL=/path/to/model.gguf
OUT=~/inference/B70-DOCS/raw/$(date +%Y%m%d-%H%M%S)
mkdir -p "$OUT"

"$BIN" --version | tee "$OUT/version.txt"
"$BIN" --help > "$OUT/help.txt"

for DEPTH in 0 32768 65536 131072; do
  for KV in 'q8_0 q8_0' 'q8_0 q5_0' 'q8_0 q4_1' 'q5_0 q4_1'; do
    read -r K V <<< "$KV"
    TAG="d${DEPTH}-k${K}-v${V}"
    timeout 1200 "$BIN" \
      -m "$MODEL" -ngl 99 \
      -p 2048 -n 128 -d "$DEPTH" \
      -b 8192 -ub 4096 \
      -fa on -ctk "$K" -ctv "$V" \
      -r 5 -o csv \
      > "$OUT/$TAG.csv" 2> "$OUT/$TAG.stderr"
  done
done
```

If the model or build rejects `-d`, record the failure and use the build's documented depth/context mechanism. Never silently alter the test.

### 13.3 Environment-variable A/B

On b9853, preserve old variable semantics. On master, use the new `ENABLE_*` names. Test from a clean environment:

```bash
env -u GGML_SYCL_DISABLE_DNN -u GGML_SYCL_ENABLE_DNN \
    -u GGML_SYCL_DISABLE_OPT -u GGML_SYCL_ENABLE_OPT \
    "$BIN" ...
```

Then explicitly compare the supported names shown by the source/build. Record startup logs proving which path activated.

### 13.4 SYCL versus Vulkan

Build Vulkan separately and run identical model, weight file, KV, prompt/depth, batch and repetitions. Do not compare across different GGUF files.

Minimum controls:

- Dense Qwen 27B Q4_K/Q5_K.
- MoE Qwen 35B Q4_K and Q4_0.
- MXFP4_MOE.
- Same 2K, 32K and 128K depth.

### 13.5 Concurrency

Use `llama-server`, not `llama-bench`, for request concurrency. Run:

- parallel 1, 4, 8, 16, 32.
- Fresh independent prompts.
- Shared-prefix prompts.
- Short prompt/long generation.
- Long prompt/short generation.

Report separately:

- request/s,
- output tok/s aggregate,
- per-request p50/p95 TTFT,
- p50/p95 inter-token latency,
- server-log pure decode,
- cache hit/reuse rate,
- VRAM peak.

The LocalMaxxing/Level1Tech 200–500+ tok/s figures are concurrency results and are not equivalent to interactive single-request speed.

## 16. Quality and correctness gate

### Mandatory layers

1. **Backend operations**
   - `MUL_MAT_ID`
   - `MUL_MAT`
   - `FLASH_ATTN_EXT`
2. **Perplexity** on a fixed corpus.
3. **Next-token distribution comparison**
   - mean KL,
   - p95 KL,
   - top-1 agreement.
4. **Long-context retrieval**
   - 8K, 32K, 64K, 128K,
   - multiple needle positions,
   - distractor-heavy prompts.
5. **Multi-turn cache consistency**
   - at least 20 turns,
   - compare cached conversation to full-prompt replay.
6. **Agent/tool behavior**
   - exact tool name,
   - JSON arguments,
   - no tool call when unnecessary,
   - recovery after tool error.
7. **Structured output**
   - strict JSON Schema validation.
8. **Executable coding**
   - deterministic tasks with unit tests.
9. **Multilingual**
   - English and Spanish.
10. **Vision**
   - OCR, diagram and UI understanding when mmproj/vision is enabled.
11. **Failure detectors**
   - NaN/Inf,
   - repetition,
   - gibberish,
   - truncation,
   - malformed reasoning/tool delimiters.

### Initial promotion thresholds

Use b9853 q8/q8 or a higher-precision supported path as reference. Initial thresholds are conservative and can be refined after distributions are visible:

- No backend-op failures.
- No deterministic-task regression.
- Relative perplexity increase <=1% for a daily quant/cache candidate.
- Mean KL <=0.02 and p95 KL <=0.10, unless task success proves a tighter threshold is needed.
- Tool/JSON success no worse than one failure in 100 versus reference.
- Long-context retrieval no loss at the intended context.
- No corruption during 100 requests and a multi-hour soak.

A result that exceeds speed targets but fails any corruption gate is rejected.

## 19. Promotion and “huge win” definition

A candidate can enter the daily service only if it passes correctness and one of:

- >=5% repeatable single-stream decode gain,
- >=15% long-context prefill gain,
- >=10% aggregate-throughput gain at the target concurrency,
- materially more context/concurrency with no quality regression,
- materially lower energy per completed task.

A **huge win** is:

- >=30% end-to-end improvement on a real daily workload,
- >=1.5x long-context prefill with unchanged quality,
- or a new quality tier/model that fits and remains responsive on one B70.

The #25312 submitted 2.48x prefill result qualifies as a huge upstream signal, but it is not a local win until reproduced with the actual model/KV configuration.

## Final recommendation

Do not begin with a model download or an upgrade. First identify the host/interface and collect failure evidence. Then freeze b9853 and compare current master KQPI=1 versus KQPI=2 before accepting any MoE result. The first serious performance experiment is #25312 with q8/q8, q8/q5_0 and the current q8/q4_1 across TILE/MKL/HYBRID; direct code inspection says q8/q4_1 is hybrid-eligible, but it is not yet benchmarked. The first alternate-runtime experiment is the officially B70-tested OpenVINO Qwen3.6-35B INT4 recipe. The first new coding-model challenger is Ornith-1.0-35B Q4_K_M. The first format/concurrency control is native gpt-oss-20B MXFP4, followed by Qwen35 MXFP4_MOE. Keep dense Qwen27 + local MTP as the rollback quality route until every replacement passes backend ops, multi-turn, coding, retrieval, tool/JSON and soak gates.
